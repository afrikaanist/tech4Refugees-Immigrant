Why we exist

Digital equity begins with lived experience.

People navigating a new country often face essential systems that assume reliable internet, strong English, technical confidence and familiarity with local institutions. Tech4RIM is building practical, multilingual and community-informed ways to close those gaps.

Our focus

Three connected paths to opportunity

We combine technology, trusted relationships and community knowledge so people can find information, build skills and shape the tools intended for them.

01

Digital access & skills

Plain-language support that helps community members use devices, online services and digital tools with confidence.

02

Resource navigation

Community-informed pathways to education, health, employment and social services—designed for multilingual access.

03

Youth leadership

Opportunities for young people to learn, create, mentor and help define more inclusive technology.

Featured projectCommunity resource prototype

From community need to a working concept

Tech4RIM sponsored a Seattle University student capstone team to explore a multilingual digital resource-navigation experience. The prototype is preserved as a foundation for future community testing and development.
