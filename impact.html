Project spotlight

T4I resource-navigation prototype

Role
Community sponsor
Collaborators
Seattle University student capstone team
Focus
Multilingual resource navigation

A student team explored how technology could make support easier to find.

Tech4RIM sponsored Seattle University students to develop a prototype for connecting refugees and immigrants with useful information and services. The work explored language access, resource categories and an interface intended for people navigating unfamiliar systems.

The prototype is not a finished public service. It is a preserved learning asset that can support future community testing, content verification and responsible development.
