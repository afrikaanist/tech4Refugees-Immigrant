Privacy

Your information should be treated with care.

This notice explains the limited role of the public Tech4RIM website.

Last updated: September 20, 2026

Public website

This is an informational website hosted through GitHub Pages. Tech4RIM does not place participant databases, case notes, banking information or confidential records on this website or in its public code repository.

Contact form

The contact form prepares a message in your own email application. It does not transmit or store your message on this website. Your email provider’s privacy practices apply when you send the message.

Donations

When secure online donations become available, payment information will be handled by the selected payment processor and not stored in this public website repository.

Stories and photographs

Tech4RIM intends to publish testimonials or photographs only with clear permission. People may decline publicity without losing access to programs or services.

Hosting information

GitHub may collect technical information, including visitors’ IP addresses, for security. Review the GitHub Privacy Statement for details.

Questions

Email contact@tech4refugeesandimmigrants.org with questions about this notice.
