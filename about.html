About Tech4RIM

Built from lived experience. Designed for shared opportunity.

Tech4RIM is a Seattle-based community initiative working at the intersection of technology, public health, education and immigrant justice.

Our story

The idea started with lived experience.

Founder Afrikaan Sahra understands personally how access to information, education and opportunity can depend on whether technology is available, understandable and designed for the people using it.

Afrikaan studied computer science and business at Seattle University and later pursued graduate study in public health at the University of Washington. Tech4Refugees & Immigrants began in 2021 to connect technology, community advocacy and the knowledge refugees and immigrants already hold.

The public name Tech4RIM now explicitly includes refugees, immigrants and migrants. The mission remains grounded in a simple principle: communities should help shape the tools and systems that affect their lives.

Our commitments

These principles guide program design, partnerships, data collection and public storytelling.

Community first

We begin with lived experience, listen before designing and build with—not only for—community members.

Useful over flashy

Technology matters when it solves a real problem, works on everyday devices and is understandable.

Language is access

Multilingual communication and plain language are essential parts of equitable design.

Dignity & consent

People control whether and how their information, image and story are shared.
